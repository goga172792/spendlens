SpendLens

Your bank statements, understood.

SpendLens is an iPhone app that reads the statements your banks email you (Ameriabank, IDBank, Inecobank, Evocabank and Byblos Bank) and turns them into a clear picture, without any typing.

This month's payments

Loans, house and car costs, and subscriptions, with the next due date first. Tick off what you've paid; your statements confirm it later.

Every loan in one place

What you borrowed, what's left, what you've paid so far, the interest still ahead, and each payment date.

Where your money goes

Spending by month and category, money in, transfers between accounts, and each statement on its own page.

Private by design

SpendLens reads your Gmail read-only, and only the emails from your banks that have statements attached. It reads them on your iPhone and keeps the results there. There are no SpendLens servers, ads or analytics. Read the privacy policy.
